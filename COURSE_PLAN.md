## How Each Lesson Works

Each of Lessons 1–8 follows four stages:
1. **See It** — Drag points on a true 1:1 coordinate grid to see the geometry in plain English before attaching terms.
2. **Worked Example** — Alex's actual classroom examples, starting with a numbered `Plan:` box and stepping through the algebra alongside the live diagram.
3. **Guided Practice** — Real problems from Handouts W2 & W4 and the Chapter 2 Test, plotting each solved step onto the diagram with live keyboard math previews and hints.
4. **Try It Yourself** — Independent practice where the `Plan:` box starts hidden behind an optional toggle.

Press **`F`** at any time to open the **Unit 2 Formula Sheet** (organized for Thursday's open-notes assignment).
